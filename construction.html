FAQ

Before you get in touch

How quickly can I get a quote?

After a site visit, a fixed-scope quote usually takes five working days. Larger civil jobs may need a soil report first, and we will say so at the start.

Can you build to my own architect's drawings?

Yes. We build to your design team's drawings, or bring in our design partners if you have not appointed anyone yet.

How do I follow progress?

Your dashboard shows percent complete, weekly photos, site documents and upcoming milestones for every active project.

What happens after handover?

Every project carries a twelve-month defects window. Report an issue in the dashboard and a site manager responds within two working days.

Have a plot, a plan or a problem site?

Tell us where it is and what you need built. A site manager will reply within one working day.
