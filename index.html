Before You Reach Out

Frequently asked questions

The most common questions we hear from prospective clients — ask us anything else directly.

How is Joma different from a law firm or accounting firm?+
We sit between the two — most of our work is tax-first, but contracts and tax positions are usually tangled together in practice. Rather than referring you elsewhere for the contract half of a tax question, one team handles both.
Can you help if I'm already mid-dispute with FIRS?+
Yes — Tax Controversy & Resolution handles active disputes, audits, and appeals. The sooner you bring us in, the more options are usually still open, but we take on cases already in progress regularly.
Do you handle both personal and company tax filings?+
Both. Individual Tax Services covers personal returns and family filings; Corporate & Business Tax handles company returns, partnerships, and group structures.
How fast can you turn around a contract review?+
Standard commercial contracts typically come back within 48 hours. More complex agreements — cross-border terms, transfer pricing arrangements — usually take longer, and we'll tell you a realistic timeline upfront.
What happens after I book a consultation?+
A department head confirms your slot, typically within one business day. From there, documents, notes, and status updates all stay visible under My Requests in the client dashboard.
